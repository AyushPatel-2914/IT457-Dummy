## B. Netflix-like workload

Netflix runs its control plane on AWS but delivers video from its own CDN (Open Connect). We price both halves separately.

### B1. Control plane: monthly cost

Per average vCPU: 0.05 TB stored and 0.05 TB of API egress per month. Cloud pays for average demand plus 30% headroom, with the always-on floor on a 1-year commitment, and needs a quarter of the operations staff per server. On-prem must own servers for peak demand plus 30% headroom (128 vCPU, $25k, 5-year life), with 1 engineer per 40 servers (minimum 1).

**Demand profile: variable (peak 3x avg)**

| Scale | AWS | GCP | On-prem | Cheapest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| small (8 vCPU avg) | $2,883 | $2,891 | $11,794 | AWS |
| medium (200 vCPU avg) | $12,067 | $12,191 | $15,099 | AWS |
| large (5,000 vCPU avg) | $235,993 | $237,820 | $139,774 | On-prem |

AWS and on-prem break even at about **308 average vCPU**.

**Demand profile: steady (peak 1.2x avg)**

| Scale | AWS | GCP | On-prem | Cheapest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| small (8 vCPU avg) | $2,841 | $2,839 | $11,794 | GCP |
| medium (200 vCPU avg) | $11,016 | $10,878 | $12,517 | GCP |
| large (5,000 vCPU avg) | $209,713 | $205,003 | $57,387 | On-prem |

AWS and on-prem break even at about **240 average vCPU**.

### B2. Video delivery: monthly cost

Cloud: CDN list price per GB with volume tiers. Own CDN: one $15k appliance per 20 Gbps of peak (4-year life, $300/month space and power), placed inside ISPs so only 30% of peak traffic uses paid transit, minimum 1 engineer.

| Volume | AWS CloudFront | GCP Cloud CDN | Own CDN | Cheapest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 TB/month | $870 | $819 | $11,725 | GCP |
| 1 PB/month | $39,987 | $34,816 | $12,138 | Own CDN |
| 100 PB/month | $2,088,724 | $3,076,096 | $125,824 | Own CDN |

CloudFront and an own CDN break even at about **187 TB per month**.
